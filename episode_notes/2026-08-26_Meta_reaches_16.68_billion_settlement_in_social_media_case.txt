Meta reaches $16.68 billion settlement in social media case

2026-08-26

Por Daniel Howley

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/meta-reaches-1668-billion-settlement-in-social-media-case-134220470.html

Meta (META) agreed to settle a social media addiction case with a group of state attorneys general on Wednesday, ending the trial in its second week and sending Meta's stock higher.

The California lawsuit is just the latest in a string of suits Meta faces over its impact on teens and young users. The company has already lost two cases, one in Los Angeles and another in New Mexico, both of which the company says it will appeal.

The cases are all unique but generally focus on how Meta designs its products rather than on the content they host. That's an important distinction, because Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act shields Meta and other internet-based platforms from liability for what their users post.
