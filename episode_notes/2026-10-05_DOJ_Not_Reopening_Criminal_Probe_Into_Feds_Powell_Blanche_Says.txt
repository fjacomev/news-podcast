DOJ Not Reopening Criminal Probe Into Fed’s Powell, Blanche Says

2026-10-05

Por Myles Miller; Chris Strohm

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/doj-not-reopening-criminal-probe-185704774.html

(Bloomberg) -- The US Justice Department isn't reopening a criminal investigation into former Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell for cost overruns related to the central bank's building renovation project.

Most Read from Bloomberg

Attorney General Todd Blanche didn't rule out continuing to look into the project's oversight and potentially take action if evidence of wrongdoing came to light.

"We're not reopening a criminal investigation into him," Blanche said in an exclusive interview with Bloomberg Friday.

Blanche commented on a report issued by the Fed's Inspector General this week that found no evidence of criminal wrongdoing by Powell connected to the $2.4 billion project. Blanche said he was satisfied with the report, but that Powell hadn't appropriately overseen the project.

The Fed has said it will engage an independent auditor to scrutinize the building costs. Blanche added that if that review finds any evidence of criminal wrongdoing, the Justice Department could investigate.
