Pepsi vs. Coca-Cola: Wall Street has clearly chosen its favorite soda stock

2026-10-06

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/pepsi-vs-coca-cola-wall-street-has-clearly-chosen-its-favorite-soda-stock-172400992.html

Wall Street has decided which beverage giant will win the year, if not for all of 2027.

Coca-Cola (KO) stock has ripped 23% higher this year, making it the eighth-best performer on the Dow (^DJI), per Yahoo Finance AlphaSpace data. PepsiCo (PEP) shares, meanwhile, have tanked by 13% and now hover near a 52-week low.

PepsiCo's earnings report on Thursday is a critical one for the beverage and snacks icon. It has promised stronger snacking results after price cuts and that its aggressive cost cuts will show up in profits.

Overall, PepsiCo has simply promised a better second half of the year than the weak first half.

The thing is, PepsiCo may not deliver on its top-line promises because of pressure on the snacks business and beverage market share losses to Coca-Cola and up-and-comers like Olipop.

Higher inflation stands to pressure any cost savings too. We are not alone in thinking this, as several Wall Street shops, including Evercore ISI and JPMorgan, have cut their profit estimates for PepsiCo ahead of the results.

What food giants General Mills and McCormick said about inflation over the past two weeks is not helping PepsiCo investor sentiment. Both reports were soft on the financials as well.

"With the stock down ~25% from February highs, we believe investors are concerned by: 1) Share losses in NAB [North America Beverages], with brand Pepsi flat over 3Q vs. brand Coke +7% and lackluster trends in PFNA [PepsiCo Foods North America], with limited improvement in trends despite the company's portfolio actions," Evercore ISI analyst Robert Ottenstein said.

"For the stock to work, we believe investors need to see improvement in PBNA [PepsiCo Beverages North America] and sustained volume growth in PFNA," Ottenstein added.
