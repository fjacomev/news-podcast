Netflix stock falls as executive shake-up aims to revive viewership growth

2026-10-16

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/netflix-stock-falls-as-executive-shake-up-aims-to-revive-viewership-growth-135228949.html

Netflix (NFLX) stock fell in early trading on Friday after the streaming giant announced a leadership shake-up amid slowing viewer engagement.

Former Disney executive Peter Rice was named as Netflix's next chief content officer, replacing Bela Bajaria, who is departing after nearly a decade at the streamer.

The move comes ahead of the company's quarterly results next week as it seeks to reinvigorate viewership growth and strengthen its content amid intensifying competition.

Earlier this week, Netflix said it will cut 6% of its workforce, or about 1,000 employees, marking the company's largest reduction of employees since 2022.

The changes at the streaming giant come amid a generational shift that is reshaping viewing habits, with audiences increasingly moving away from traditional television sets toward mobile devices, noted Piper Sandler director and senior research analyst Tom Champion.

Netflix is expected to report quarterly results next Tuesday, Oct. 20, after the market close.

Ines Ferre is a senior business reporter for Yahoo Finance.
