Walmart stock moves higher after Deutsche Bank upgrades to Buy, raises price target

2026-10-16

Por Brooke DiPalma

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/walmart-stock-moves-higher-after-deutsche-bank-upgrades-to-buy-raises-price-target-135455981.html

In an ongoing inflationary environment, Walmart (WMT) is poised to win, according to Deutsche Bank analyst Krisztina Katai, who upgraded the stock to a Buy rating from Neutral on Thursday morning.

"We believe the current inflationary environment should reinforce Walmart's value proposition and support accelerated share gains, while we think investors are starting to increasingly look beyond the headline pressure on U.S. comp.," Katai wrote in a note to clients.

According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics' September Consumer Price Index, overall inflation rose 3.7% in September, an acceleration from 3.4% in August. Grocery prices increased by 1.8%, but were roughly flat month over month.

Walmart stock popped 3% in premarket trading.

In the second quarter, the company posted its slowest US same-store sales growth since Q4 of 2020, at 2.6%.

Katai said in a meeting with CFO John David Rainey that the company noted consumer trends were stable to improving compared to three months ago, while price gaps remain strong compared to its competitors.

Grocery still accounts for the majority of Walmart's US sales, but general merchandise, including apparel, represents a growing part of the business, and the percentage of sales coming from private-label brands increased by 1.3 percentage points year over year.

That momentum seems to be holding, with strong gains in food, e-commerce, and its marketplace. Higher-profit businesses, like advertising and membership fees, are also expected to boost margins.

As of late, the retail giant has leaned into its fashion offerings with private-label brands Scoop, Free Assembly, and Scenario as part of a bet to attract the deeper wallets of higher-income customers
