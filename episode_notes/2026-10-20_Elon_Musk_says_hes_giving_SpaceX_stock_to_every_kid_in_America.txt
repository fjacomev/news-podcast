Elon Musk says he's giving SpaceX stock to every kid in America

2026-10-20

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/elon-musk-says-hes-giving-spacex-stock-to-every-kid-in-america-131435467.html

Kids will soon have a financial interest in the future of space.

"Next year, I will give one of my SpaceX (SPCX) shares to every kid in America. This will be the first of many," SpaceX founder and CEO Elon Musk said in an X post on Monday.

Musk didn't specify an age range for the proposed gift, but a Bloomberg report indicated he could use Trump Accounts as the vehicle for his stock donations.

SpaceX stock rose 1% in morning trading on Monday.

There are currently about 73 million children under 18 living in the US, according to the latest Census Bureau data. That number goes down to about 18.5 million for newborns up to 4 years old.

SpaceX COO Gwynne Shotwell has already pledged a personal donation of more than $320 million in SpaceX stock to fund Trump Accounts for more than 2 million children.
