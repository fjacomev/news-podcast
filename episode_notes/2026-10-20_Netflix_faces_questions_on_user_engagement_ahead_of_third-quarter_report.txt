Netflix faces questions on user engagement ahead of third-quarter report

2026-10-20

Por Reuters

https://finance.yahoo.com/media-advertising/articles/netflix-faces-questions-user-engagement-101735340.html

Oct 19 (Reuters) - Netflix investors are expecting details on how the streaming giant plans to sustain growth when it reports third-quarter results on Tuesday, as competition intensifies and user engagement shows signs of fatigue.

Ted Sarandos, the co-CEO of Netflix, recently admitted the company "is not growing as fast as I want us to", and suggested live programming could be an opportunity to accelerate growth.

Here are more details:

• Netflix is expected to post its slowest third-quarter revenue growth in three years, with sales forecast to increase 11.8% to $12.87 billion, according to data compiled by LSEG.

• It cut about 6% of its workforce in a move that analyst Ross Benes of Emarketer said reflected the streaming industry's shift to profitability.

• Netflix also saw a change in its content leadership, with longtime head of content Bela Bajaria leaving the company and former Disney executive Peter Rice taking over.

• Its US user churn rose 16.6% year-over-year in the third quarter, roughly double the pace of the prior three quarters, while downloads in the US and Canada fell 20%, according to market research firm Apptopia.

• Jefferies estimates average monthly viewing time per Netflix member fell to 49 hours in the first half of this year from 66 hours in the first half of 2023.

• The 2026 FIFA World Cup and a weaker content slate than last year weighed on Netflix's engagement, analysts said.

• The company recently acquired broadcast rights to air the 2030 FIFA World Cup in Spain and Andorra.

• Netflix is betting on advertising to diversify revenue, with Jefferies forecasting ad revenue to reach $7.5 billion in 2029.

(Reporting by Harshita Mary Varghese in Bengaluru; Editing by Edmund Lee and Devika Syamnath)
