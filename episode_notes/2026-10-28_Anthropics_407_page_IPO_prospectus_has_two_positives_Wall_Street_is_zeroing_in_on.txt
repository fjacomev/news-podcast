Many on Wall Street concede they haven't yet read all 407 pages of the newly filed IPO prospectus from Anthropic (ANTH.PVT), which dropped Monday.

But a consensus is forming around two things the Street likes in what it has read: revenue diversification and revenue that outpaces compute costs.

The prospectus shows Anthropic's revenue base became more diversified this year, with customers outside the top five now making up about 79% of sales, up from 64% in the first quarter. In its simplest form, the metric points to wider adoption of Anthropic's technology.

Meanwhile, compute and infrastructure spending reached $8.9 billion in the first half of this year. Although that exceeds the total for all of 2025, it fell to 55% of sales from 201% in the first half of 2025.

"Given revenue is outpacing compute growth and cash burn year to date has been modest, we believe these will likely be viewed by investors as positive early indicators of profit potential," said KeyBanc analyst Justin Patterson.

Anthropic's revenue for the first six months of 2026 surged to $16.3 billion from $1.1 billion a year earlier, the prospectus shows. The filing also discloses an $80.7 billion net loss in the same period, a strong signal of aggressive AI spending in the years ahead.

Investment bankers are reportedly aiming for an astronomical listing valuation of roughly $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion.

Anthropic could debut on public markets before the end of November.
