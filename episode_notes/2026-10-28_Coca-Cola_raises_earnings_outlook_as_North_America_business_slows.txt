Coca-Cola raises earnings outlook as North America business slows

2026-10-28

Por Brooke DiPalma

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/coca-cola-raises-earnings-outlook-as-north-america-business-slows-115001447.html

Coca-Cola (KO) posted better-than-expected third quarter results and raised its earnings outlook, but the company saw slowing demand in its North American business. The stock traded lower on Tuesday.

The company posted adjusted earnings of $0.91 per share, above the $0.88 expected by Wall Street analysts, alongside revenue of $13.4 billion, also higher than estimates of $12.9 billion.

Overall unit case volume grew by 5%, more than the roughly 3% expected. The increase was once again led by Coke Zero, which grew roughly 16% year over year. Diet Coke and Coca-Cola Light grew by 6%.

However, higher input and operating costs weighed on Coca-Cola's results in its main North American market.

CEO Henrique Braun said that some of the softness was due to the Fourth of July falling into the second quarter and to supply issues for Topo Chico that are now being fixed.

"In a nutshell, if you take it out, those two elements, we would be growing, and we see that was a solid result," he told Yahoo Finance, adding that Coke Zero grew by double digits.

Braun said the company has been able to hedge against higher commodity costs and geopolitical disruptions. Coca-Cola is leaning into different pack sizes to offer various price points for consumers and also premium, higher-priced options like Smartwater and its protein drink Core Power in flavors like banana, which it plans to bring to the US market soon.

Despite cost pressures, the company raised its full-year earnings guidance yet again.

Coca-Cola now expects earnings growth of 11%, compared to a prior range of 9% to 10%. Before that, Coca-Cola previously expected earnings growth in the 8% to 9% range.

"We got the momentum … from [the] first half of the year into the third quarter, and when we look at the momentum that we have, we felt that was the right update to make, and we feel confident that together with our bottling partners, we have already [what] it takes to deliver … updated guidance," Braun said.

In the quarter, the company also struck a deal with Yum! Brands (YUM) subsidiary KFC to be its beverage provider for thousands of its locations. PepsiCo (PEP) will reportedly still supply KFC internationally.

"Together with the KFC team, we're definitely going to work on personalized, customized experiences that can help them to continue to grow their business and us together," he added.
