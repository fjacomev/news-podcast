Home prices continued to climb in August before mortgage rates surged

2026-10-28

Por Claire Boston

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/home-prices-continued-to-climb-in-august-before-mortgage-rates-surged-145452131.html

Home price appreciation accelerated again in August while remaining below inflation.

The S&P Cotality Case-Shiller 20-City Composite Home Price Index, which measures home prices in 20 of the nation's largest metropolitan areas, rose 2.65% in August, from 2.47% in July.

The national index, which measures prices in a broader group of cities, rose 2.09%. The latest figures cover transactions that took place in June, July, and August, before mortgage rates crossed 7% and chilled sales.

The gains once again fell short of inflation, which hit 3.4% in August due to higher gas prices. Home prices have been falling in inflation-adjusted terms for the past 15 months, though they remain out of reach for many prospective buyers. The median home cost $429,100 in August, according to National Association of Realtors data, a record high for that month.

"Affordability … remains challenging," Rebecca Kaufman, associate director of commodities at S&P Dow Jones Indices, said in a statement. "With inflation at 3.4%, Brent crude near $100 per barrel and mortgage rates in the mid-to-high 6% range, households continue to face elevated costs alongside persistently high home prices."

Home price appreciation — or lack thereof — continues to vary widely across individual cities. Home prices in Chicago rose 6.7% in August, the fastest of any major US metro area. New York home prices rose 6.2%, while Cleveland's were up 4.3%. But home prices in Seattle fell 2%.

Claire Boston is a senior reporter for Yahoo Finance covering housing, mortgages, and home insurance.
