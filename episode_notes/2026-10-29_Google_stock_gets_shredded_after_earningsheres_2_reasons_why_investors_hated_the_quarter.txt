Google stock gets shredded after earnings—here's 2 reasons why investors hated the quarter

2026-10-29

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/google-stock-gets-shredded-after-earningsheres-2-reasons-why-investors-hated-the-quarter-090019155.html

Sizzling Google Cloud growth of 103% isn't going to cut it for Alphabet (GOOGL) bulls the day after earnings.

Alphabet shares tanked 10% in pre-market trading on Thursday after a third quarter that could best be characterized as a letdown.

The Street is locked in on two aspects of the quarter.

First, Google's search business notched sales of $63.4 billion, below the $64.9 billion expected. The miss fueled fears Google's search empire is being chipped away by the likes of Claude and ChatGPT, despite massive investment by Google.

Second, Google posted negative free cash flow of $9.1 billion versus a Wall Street estimate for negative $3.5 billion. In a market getting jittery about negative free cash flow at Big Tech (see the reaction to Meta's (META) results last week, when it also had negative free cash flow for the first time in years), Google's print stunk up the joint.

Overall, Alphabet delivered a rare double-miss in its third-quarter earnings report, posting $127.57 billion in revenue against Wall Street's $128.2 billion consensus estimate.

Bottom-line results disappointed significantly as earnings per share fell to $2.12, badly missing analyst projections of $3.02 per share.
