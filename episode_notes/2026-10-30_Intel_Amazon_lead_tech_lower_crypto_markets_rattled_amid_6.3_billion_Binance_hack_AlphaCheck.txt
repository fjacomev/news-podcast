Good morning. Stocks declined on Friday as shares of chipmaker Intel (INTC) and Amazon (AMZN) led Technology (XLK) and Consumer Discretionary (XLY) stocks lower following their quarterly results.

The declines came after Alphabet (GOOG, GOOGL) stock plunged 12% in the prior session.

Here's a check of the markets in the first few minutes of trading, based on a heat map powered by Yahoo Finance AlphaSpace data.

Crypto markets were rattled on Friday after Binance reported a $6.3 billion breach in what is likely the largest heist in history.

Bitcoin (BTC-USD) prices tumbled to around $77,000. Ether (ETH-USD) also dropped 4% to around $2,200. Crypto-related stocks also opened lower. Shares of Circle (CRCL) and trading platforms Coinbase (COIN) and Robinhood (HOOD) all tumbled. Digital-asset Treasury giant Strategy (MSTR) stock also sank.
