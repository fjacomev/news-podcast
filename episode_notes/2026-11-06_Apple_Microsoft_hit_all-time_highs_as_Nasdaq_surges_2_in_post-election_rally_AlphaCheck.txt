Good morning. Stocks surged on Wednesday, with most sectors green across the board after a Democratic mid-term election sweep. Oil prices extended their decline amid growing hopes for a diplomatic resolution to the war in the Middle East. Brent crude oil futures (BZ=F) tumbled more than 2% to around $100 per barrel, while WTI (CL=F) also dropped, hovering above $93 per barrel.
