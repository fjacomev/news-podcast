Cloudflare shares leap after blowout Q3 results, strong forecast

2026-11-06

Por Luke Juricic

https://finance.yahoo.com/news/cloudflare-shares-soar-hours-blowout-215805051.html

Investing.com -- Cloudflare Inc (NYSE:NET) shares surged more than 14% in premarket trading Friday after the connectivity and cloud security provider delivered third-quarter results that surpassed Wall Street expectations across all primary metrics and issued an aggressive full-year outlook.

The robust performance was underpinned by accelerating enterprise adoption and surging demand for the company's developer platform amid broader artificial intelligence workloads.

Revenue for the quarter ended September 30 climbed 39% year-over-year to $782.5 million, topping the consensus estimate of $736.8 million and marking the company's fastest pace of top-line expansion in nearly four years. On an adjusted basis, net income reached $0.38 per diluted share, beating analyst projections of $0.34 and improving from $0.27 per share in the prior-year period.

Operational indicators underscored broad-based commercial momentum, highlighted by a 37% year-over-year increase in current remaining performance obligations (cRPO). Adjusted operating income expanded to $137.1 million, or 17.5% of total revenue, up from $85.9 million, or 15.3% of revenue, a year earlier.

On a GAAP basis, operating loss widened to $67.9 million from $37.5 million in the third quarter of 2025, driven in part by $174.1 million in stock-based compensation and $11.0 million in restructuring charges. GAAP net loss totaled $34.1 million, or $0.10 per share, compared to a net loss of $1.3 million in the prior-year period.

Cash generation remained steady despite heightened infrastructure investments, with operating cash flow rising to $215.4 million from $167.1 million a year ago. Free cash flow came in at $82.1 million, representing a 10% margin, while total cash, cash equivalents, and available-for-sale securities stood at $5.13 billion at quarter-end.

Management highlighted structural tailwinds tied to security and AI infrastructure as primary growth catalysts. "We crossed $3 billion in annualized revenue with revenue of $782 million, up 39% year over year," co-founder and chief executive Matthew Prince said in the release, adding that the company has "built the development platform for the AI-powered Internet."
