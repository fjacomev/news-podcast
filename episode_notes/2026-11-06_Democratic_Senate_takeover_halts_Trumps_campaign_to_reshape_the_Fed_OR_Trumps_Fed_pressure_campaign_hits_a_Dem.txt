Democratic Senate takeover halts Trump’s campaign to reshape the Fed OR Trump’s Fed pressure campaign hits a Democratic wall in the Senate

2026-11-06

Por Jennifer Schonberger

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/trumps-fed-pressure-campaign-hits-a-new-democratic-wall-in-the-senate-100000283.html

Democrats secured control of the Senate on Tuesday, a seismic shift that effectively blocks President Trump's campaign to reshape the Federal Reserve and neutralizes what many analysts viewed as a threat to the central bank's independence.

It also heightens the challenge for Trump should he maneuver to replace any sitting officials.

"Democrats' control of the Senate reclaims the party's ability to approve or deny the President's nominees for judges and heads of other executive branch agencies," Jonathan Pingle, an economist for UBS, wrote in a note. "That includes the need for Democrats to approve any new members of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System should one of the existing governors step aside."

That political firewall directly impacts the administration's ongoing effort to remove Fed governor Lisa Cook. Trump fired Cook in August 2025, claiming she committed mortgage fraud by making misrepresentations on loan documents years before her appointment to the central bank. Cook, a Biden appointee, has denied any wrongdoing and sued. The lawsuit has gone through multiple court rungs before reaching the Supreme Court earlier this year.

Last month, Trump established a committee to determine whether her statements constituted legal "cause" for removal from the central bank, signaling that his efforts to oust her are continuing.

However, Tuesday's election significantly changed the stakes of that investigation. Michael Feroli, chief US economist for JPMorgan, noted that the Senate flip alters the math for the White House.

"Even a successful removal would not open a seat up to a friend of the administration," Feroli wrote in a client note.

Jaret Seiberg, analyst with TD Cowen, agreed that the inability to confirm a replacement may make Trump less inclined to fire Cook.

Not everyone is convinced the administration will back down, however. Loretta Mester, former head of the Cleveland Fed, told Yahoo Finance, "Perhaps it makes it less likely he will succeed, but I'm not convinced it will stop the president from trying."

More broadly, Democrats taking power in Congress changes the atmosphere in which Fed officials work to set monetary policy devoid of political influence.

Trump has been vocal and persistent in urging policymakers to lower interest rates. The central bank has held steady, raising rates at times despite the White House pressure. The new power dynamic gives them more cover.
