Nvidia, AMD, and Micron stocks lead chip sector higher following worst sell-off since 2020

2026-11-06

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-amd-micron-sandsik-lead-chip-sector-higher-134336542.html

Chip stocks rebounded on Friday after suffering their worst sell-off since 2020. The PHLX Semiconductor Index (^SOX) bounced 4% following a 12% plunge on Thursday.

Shares of US chipmaker Intel (INTC) jumped about 7%, erasing part of their 10% slide in the prior session. AMD (AMD) also rebounded 11% after suffering a 17% fall. Nvidia (NVDA), which declined 9% in the prior session, jumped more than 2%.

Semiconductors bounced back after much of the AI trade was hammered on Thursday as investors digested what a Democratic win in Congress means for the data center build-out in the US.

Memory and storage names Sandisk (SNDK), Micron Technology (MU) and SK Hynix (SKHY) also rebounded.

Other notable movers rebounding on Friday include processor maker Qualcomm (QCOM) and chip designer Arm Holdings (ARM).
