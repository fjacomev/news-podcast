'Pretty solid job growth:' Private US employers added 86,000 jobs in October

2026-11-06

Por Claire Boston

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/pretty-solid-job-growth-private-us-employers-added-86000-jobs-in-october-140029435.html

Hiring in the private sector showed continued strength in October, according to payroll processor ADP, as employers across a variety of industries added roles.

Private companies added 86,000 jobs this month, matching a revised 86,000 in September, data released Wednesday showed. Economists had been expecting a gain of 67,000 jobs.

Base wages for private-sector workers rose 3.2%, a figure that still trails inflation, while gross pay, which includes additional compensation such as overtime and bonuses, was up 4.7%.

"You have back-to-back, two consecutive straight months of pretty solid job growth," ADP chief economist Nela Richardson said.

Gains in healthcare and education continue to be major drivers of hiring, with the sectors adding 28,000 jobs last month. Showing even more strength was the category of trade, transportation, and utilities, with 39,000 additions.

But there are still spots of weakness within the job market. Business services shed 27,000 roles, and information lost 2,000 jobs, a sign of ongoing challenges in certain white-collar industries.

The ADP report covers private-sector hiring and comes out ahead of the Labor Department's broader survey data on public and private employers on Friday. Economists expect the economy added 65,000 jobs in October by that measure.

Claire Boston is a senior reporter for Yahoo Finance covering housing, mortgages, and home insurance.
