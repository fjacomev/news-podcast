Hassett Says No Economic Policy Shift Even After Election Rout

2026-11-07

Por Michael Sasso

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/hassett-says-no-economic-policy-153704724.html

(Bloomberg) -- President Donald Trump's chief economic adviser said the administration won't be revamping its economic policies in the wake of Republicans' sweeping defeat in the midterm congressional elections.

"I don't think we have a policy change we need to plan," Kevin Hassett, director of the White House National Economic Council, said in an interview on Bloomberg TV Friday. "The policies are working."

Hassett argued the Democratic victory in the elections wasn't a rebuttal of the president's supply-side policies, and reiterated Trump's threat to use vetoes to keep his agenda in place. Among other benefits, those measures have led to 120,000 people involved in building new factories, Hassett said, following an October employment report showing the nation added 165,000 jobs last month.

Trump "is concerned that the positive momentum that we've built could be upended by Democrats now that they've taken more power," Hassett said. "But I think they're going to have a really hard time repealing all the great policies which created the strong job market, because the president will veto it."

Hassett attributed recent stickiness in inflation to the Iran war and its effects on gasoline prices, rather than the administration's economic policies, which he suggested are having a downward effect on inflation. He contrasted that with what he called the inflationary impact of former President Joe Biden's agenda, "where they basically helicopter-dropped money down to people after the Covid crisis was over."

Joking that he had gotten a "veto hat" from the president, Hassett said the administration also has a "huge amount" of executive authority to keep pressing ahead with its program, including taking on over-regulation.
