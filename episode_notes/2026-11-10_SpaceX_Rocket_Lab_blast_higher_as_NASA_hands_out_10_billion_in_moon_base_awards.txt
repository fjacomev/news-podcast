SpaceX, Rocket Lab blast higher as NASA hands out $10 billion in moon base awards

2026-11-10

Por Jared Blikre

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/spacex-rocket-lab-blast-higher-as-nasa-hands-out-10-billion-in-moon-base-awards-164320855.html

NASA wants a base on the Moon, and Wall Street is suddenly buying the ride there. Space stocks ripped for a second session Monday as SpaceX (SPCX) and seven other companies line up for lunar delivery work worth up to $10 billion.

NASA named the eight winners on Nov. 7 at Kennedy Space Center. Alongside SpaceX and Blue Origin are three smaller public companies, Intuitive Machines (LUNR), Firefly Aerospace (FLY) and Voyager Technologies (VOYG).

For those three, the prize is out of this world. They're worth about $8.5 billion combined, less than the contract's ceiling, though each still has to win missions one by one.

Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) is NASA's way of hiring private companies to fly cargo to the moon. The new 10-year version covers larger landers, and NASA raised the original contract's value to $4.2 billion from $2.6 billion. Intuitive Machines CEO Steve Altemus said the company is "excited to participate in this next evolution of CLPS."

"We are building the Moon Base mission by mission," NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman said in the agency's announcement.

Rocket Lab (RKLB) didn't make NASA's list but jumped anyway ahead of its third quarter results after Monday's close. The stock rose about 28% over two sessions, its third-best two-day run in two years, by Yahoo Finance calculations.

Space stocks surged off last week's post-election low, rising roughly 20% to 60% since the Nov. 5 close, with Firefly in the lead. SpaceX posted its biggest two-day gain since early October, while the Nasdaq (^IXIC) gained about 4%.

The bounce follows a brutal stretch. An average of nine space stocks more than doubled this year by May 28, two weeks before SpaceX's June 12 debut, then gave it all back. The group is now down for 2026.

SpaceX is a different story. Wall Street prices it less as a lunar trucker than as an AI cloud, competing with neoclouds — companies like CoreWeave (CRWV) that rent out AI computing power — and with hyperscalers, the cloud giants such as Amazon (AMZN) and Microsoft (MSFT).

On Monday, SpaceX added more than $100 billion in market value, more than the roughly $90 billion that 16 other US-listed space stocks are worth combined. NASA's 10-year moon pot is about a tenth of that one-day gain.

Rocket Lab's third quarter revenue jumped 73% from a year earlier to nearly $268 million, topping estimates, and its fourth quarter forecast came in above Wall Street's. Even so, the stock slipped after hours, and Neutron, its bigger rocket, isn't set to debut until 2027.
