Consumers are turning to cheaper brands like it's 2021 as inflation pinches

2026-11-11

Por Hamza Shaban

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/consumers-are-turning-to-cheaper-brands-like-its-2021-as-inflation-pinches-110000841.html

The verdict is in, or nearly there. After Tuesday's hotter-than-expected inflation numbers, the Fed is most likely going to raise interest rates at its policy meeting next month.

On top of that, investors are now worried about a much more hawkish Fed in 2027.

Pricing pressures were so strong across essentials like food, rent, and medical care that there's renewed chatter the Fed might impose a jumbo hike of 50 basis points.

The expectations have clearly changed.

Higher costs are showing up in other places too. Like in data about how Americans are spending their money. According to a new analysis by the Bank of America Institute, consumers are "trading down" to cheaper brands at the highest levels since the height of the pandemic.

The data reveals that consumers are increasingly value-conscious. People are buying store brands over name brands, going to cheaper retailers instead of department stores, and eating fast food in place of sit-down restaurants.

"While households continue to spend, they are increasingly seeking discounts as concerns about higher prices persist," wrote Taylor Bowley, an economist at the Bank of America Institute. With a ton of deposit accounts, credit cards, and debit cards, the bank is uniquely positioned to track spending across a wide swath of demographics.

The analysis showed consumers appear increasingly focused on value, shifting spending to value-tier merchants at 2021 levels. Millennials — faced with the cost of raising families, high housing, auto, and childcare costs — are leading the shift toward value-oriented shopping, the report found.

That change is most pronounced in apparel and grocery spending, suggesting that the hunt for bargains isn't limited to discretionary purchases but now extends to everyday household spending.

"While consumers can delay apparel purchases, trading down on groceries is notable because it suggests Millennials are looking for savings even in areas where spending is harder to avoid, like food," the report said.
