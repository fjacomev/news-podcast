Micron stock is a screaming buy at current valuation levels: Goldman Sachs

2026-11-11

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/micron-stock-is-a-screaming-buy-at-current-valuation-levels-goldman-sachs-172852076.html

Micron's (MU) valuation has reached a level where it's simply too cheap to ignore given its fundamentals, says Goldman Sachs analyst James Schneider.

Schneider reiterated his $1,250 price target on the once red-hot AI momentum trade. It assumes about 70% upside from current levels.

"We also believe risk/reward is becoming more attractive for Neutral-rated Micron in light of the high probability of strong free cash flow in the coming quarters," Schneider said.

Micron is currently approaching tangible book value, Schneider noted. When a stock trades below tangible book value, investors value the company at less than the estimated value of its assets after subtracting its liabilities and intangible assets.

In a sense, the market is saying the company's physical and financial assets are worth more than the entire company's stock market value.

That seems to be bordering on an unwarranted disconnect, at least in assessing Schneider's work on the chip player.

Micron's stock is down 17% over the past month amid fears that a blue wave on Election Day could slow the build-out of AI infrastructure. That could have ramifications for orders for Micron's memory chips.

The rout has brought Micron's forward price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio down to an almost laughable 4.2 times, according to Yahoo Finance AlphaSpace data. Keep in mind Micron is coming off a quarter of strong demand amid memory chip shortages driving up prices.

The Street still expects Micron to grow earnings by at least 17% in its next fiscal year.
