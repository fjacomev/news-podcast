‘An intergalactic freak show’: Republicans are piling on Howard Lutnick

2026-11-14

Por Ben Werschkul

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/lutnick-agrees-to-testify-before-congress-as-republicans-pile-on-the-embattled-commerce-secretary-151715681.html

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has reportedly agreed to testify before lawmakers next month as the spotlight on him grows harsher amid Nvidia's (NVDA) turmoil.

Both Democrats and Republicans alike are raising questions about whether Lutnick went too far in protecting Nvidia's push to sell its chips overseas even as the US government was warning about a possible security breach.

Lutnick is reportedly set to appear on Dec. 10 before the Senate Banking Committee, in news first reported by Fox News. Spokespersons for the Commerce Department and the Senate panel did not immediately provide additional details.

The appearance would set up what could be a major moment for the suddenly embattled Commerce secretary after days in which he and the White House had largely stayed silent as even some of Trump's closest allies grew more vocal.

Lutnick will appear before Republican Sen. Tim Scott of South Carolina, the chairman of the Senate panel until the end of the year and a regular on President Trump's campaign stops last month. Scott released a letter Thursday evening requesting that Lutnick and ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang appear before Congress early next month.

"Recent reports raise serious concerns about our national security and the protection of sensitive U.S. technology," Scott wrote. "The American people deserve answers."

Republican Sen. John Kennedy of Louisiana used more colorful language in a recent appearance on CNN, saying he wanted answers about why the Commerce Department allowed Nvidia to sell chips to an alleged Chinese spy's company.

"This whole thing is an intergalactic freak show," Kennedy said.

Some Trump aides have declined to defend Lutnick emphatically.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent raised eyebrows Friday during a CNBC appearance when he reiterated his desire for stronger government limits on advanced semiconductor exports — which he added that the Pentagon, State Department, and the trade representative's office agreed with.

"Commerce has the pen…," Bessent said, "and you will have to ask them about their internal processes."

The rapid-fire developments have also increased the odds of Congress passing new curbs on semiconductor exports to China, with lawmakers now moving to add some combination of three bipartisan plans into a military funding bill that needs to pass before the end of the year.

A White House official said that Trump maintains confidence in Lutnick, but the president's only direct reaction to the scandal in recent days was a Truth Social post saying Huang had "done a fantastic job" and that former Apple CEO Tim Cook should take over Nvidia.
