Fed’s Collins Says Inflation Fight Outweighs Housing Woes

2026-11-14

Por Maria Eloisa Capurro

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fed-collins-says-inflation-fight-141000214.html

(Bloomberg) -- Federal Reserve Bank of Boston President Susan Collins signaled Friday that tighter monetary policy may be needed to cool prices.

In a speech centered on the housing market, Collins said higher interest rates can limit housing affordability. Still, she added, ensuring price stability will benefit both the housing market and the overall economy in the long run.

"In the near term, the higher interest rates sometimes needed to cool the economy and keep inflation in check can make homes more expensive to buy and to build," Collins said in remarks prepared for the Boston Fed's annual economic conference. "But over the longer run, stable prices create predictable economic conditions that are good for housing."

After tightening monetary policy for the first time in three years in September, Fed officials held their benchmark rate steady last month. Several policymakers have signaled their support for a rate increase in December following hotter-than-expected inflation data for October. Investors currently anticipate hikes in December and January, with a third in either March or April, based on federal funds futures pricing.
