Nvidia stock slips as Jensen Huang reportedly weighs CEO comeback, Musk backs return

2026-11-16

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-stock-steady-as-jensen-huang-reportedly-weighs-ceo-comeback-musk-backs-return-015644730.html

Nvidia (NVDA) stock futures fell almost 2% on Monday as investors digested a report that supporters of Jensen Huang are lobbying for his return to the AI chip giant following his abrupt resignation last week.

Huang has discussed returning as CEO with confidants, the Financial Times reported Sunday, citing people familiar with the matter. Huang resigned last week following reports of affairs, including one with a woman who was reportedly suspected of being a Chinese spy.

According to the report, a Huang supporter said that a "war room" of Silicon Valley executives was being assembled to lobby Nvidia's board to reinstate him, including by seeking support from the White House.

"Everyone that matters in tech outside the frontier labs loves Jensen," the person said, adding that Huang supported the effort.

Meanwhile, SpaceX (SPCX) and Tesla (TSLA) CEO Elon Musk has come out in support of Huang, writing on X last Friday, "A petition is underway to reinstate Jensen, and I'm signing it too."

On Saturday, Musk said, "Jensen drove supply chain brilliantly at Nvidia, but many of those deals were a handshake with Jensen, not a written contract. Without Jensen, at least some of those critical deals will be lost."

The push to bring Huang back comes days after the FT reported that Nvidia's board had approached Arm Holdings (ARM) CEO Rene Haas as it searches for a successor. Nvidia CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO during the search.

Yorkville's Dan Ives said that while "no one individual can replace Jensen as CEO," any talk of "Jensen coming back to Nvidia is ridiculous in our view given the legal/Board/DC scrutiny/moral compass/China tentacles."

Huang abruptly stepped down Thursday following reports of alleged affairs with a former executive from Singapore-based Megaspeed, and also an Nvidia employee who reported directly to him.

Exactly one week earlier, Huang acknowledged being unfaithful to his wife but did not disclose how many affairs he had or identify those involved.

Recent reports have raised concerns about whether restricted semiconductors were being diverted to China and if sensitive US information had been sent to Beijing.

The developments have also sent shockwaves through the AI industry, where Nvidia has played a central role in building the ecosystem. The company has become a bellwether for the AI trade and has invested in key parts of the industry, including large language model development, energy, optics, and cloud providers.

Nvidia is scheduled to report quarterly results Tuesday after the bell, in what will be the company's first earnings call without Huang.

The stock declined more than 12% last week, closing below a $4 trillion market cap. At least 10 Wall Street analysts have downgraded the stock from Buy to Hold, with many citing, in part, increased scrutiny in Washington and succession uncertainty.
