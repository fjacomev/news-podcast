AMD finalizes $8.2 billion World Labs acquisition, adds 'godmother of AI' as EVP and chief scientist

AMD (AMD) completed its $8.2 billion acquisition of Fei-Fei Li's World Labs on Monday, giving the company access to World Labs' AI models and research.

Li will also serve as executive vice president and chief scientist at the chip giant. Considered the 'godmother of AI,' Li's research is foundational to the AI boom.

World Labs develops AI models that simulate 3D environments to train robots to maneuver in both virtual and real worlds.

"Delivering the infrastructure that will power the next era of super intelligence requires us to optimize compute platforms and models together," AMD CEO Lisa Su said in a statement.

"We are thrilled to welcome Fei-Fei Li and the exceptional World Labs team to AMD. Their pioneering model research will help shape our future hardware, software, and systems. Together, we will enable researchers and developers across the ecosystem to push the boundaries of what AI can do," she added.

Yahoo Finance named Su its CEO of the Year for 2026 during its Invest conference last week.

The acquisition will help AMD better compete in the race to develop AI models that can power robots, a space that rival Nvidia (NVDA) has grabbed a strong foothold in thanks to its collection of open-weights models.
