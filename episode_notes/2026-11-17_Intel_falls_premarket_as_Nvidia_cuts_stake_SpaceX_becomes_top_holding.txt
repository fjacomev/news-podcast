Intel falls premarket as Nvidia cuts stake, SpaceX becomes top holding

Investing.com -- Intel shares fell more than 3% in premarket trading Tuesday after Nvidia significantly trimmed its stake in the chipmaker.

The move comes as part of a broader portfolio reshuffle from Nvidia. The AI darling increased its stake in SpaceX while significantly scaling back its exposure to Intel, Nokia, and Synopsys.

The AI bellwether Nvidia raised its SpaceX holding to 130.5M shares as of September 30, up from 122.8M shares at the end of June. At the same time, it cut its Intel position by 25% to 161.1M shares from 214.8M shares.

The move puts SpaceX ahead of Intel as Nvidia's largest disclosed equity investment. Its SpaceX stake was valued at around $19.7 billion at the end of Q3, slightly above the $19.4 billion value of its remaining Intel holding. The two investments now make up more than 80% of Nvidia's $48.3B disclosed portfolio.

SpaceX was the only one of the eight stocks in Nvidia's filing in which it reported owning more shares.

Nvidia also pulled back on its investments in Nokia and Synopsys, trimming those positions by 56% and 10%, respectively.
