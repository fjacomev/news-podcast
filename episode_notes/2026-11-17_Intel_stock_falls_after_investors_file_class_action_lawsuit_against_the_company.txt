Intel stock falls after investors file class action lawsuit against the company

2026-11-17

Por Paige Oamek

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/intel-stock-falls-after-investors-file-class-action-lawsuit-against-the-company-155514379.html

Intel (INTC) stock fell more than 6% Monday morning as the market reacted to news that the company is facing a class action lawsuit from investors.

In the lawsuit filed on Friday, Intel shareholders claim the technology company misled investors about the impact of a slowdown in PC sales on its revenue. The complaint also claims Intel hid that supply limits blocked the company from meeting data center demand.

When those realities came to light in Intel's third quarter earnings report last month, shares dropped 28.6% the next day, losing around $140 billion of market value in the company's largest single-day decline in more than 50 years

"Any positive impacts to Intel's business and financial results resulting from data center construction and other aspects of the AI boom were more than offset by the negative effects on the Company's business and financial results resulting from the PC market slowdown and the excess inventory buildup described," read the lawsuit filed by Robbins Geller Rudman & Dowd.

The complaint states that in January, CEO Lip-Bu Tan claimed Intel was prepared to capture "unprecedented demand" for its products as a result of AI-related infrastructure projects. In July, Intel executive vice president and CFO David Zinsner also said a PC slowdown would allow Intel to shift production toward data center CPUs.

However, on the October call, Zinsner said the company was also facing severe supply constraints, including a shortage of substrates, a key material used in packaging chips, which undercut that story.

The lawsuit seeks to represent those who invested in Intel between July 23 and Oct. 29 of this year.

Intel stock is down nearly 50% over the past six months, following a substantial climb earlier in the year.
