Kindness 'compounds': Warren Buffett donates $5.5 billion to family foundations

2026-11-17

Por Paige Oamek

It's the season for giving.

Billionaire Warren Buffett announced on Monday that he will give 9.225 million Class B Berkshire Hathaway (BRK-B) shares — or roughly $5.5 billion — to four philanthropic foundations this Thanksgiving.

In his annual Thanksgiving letter, Buffett said he will give 3.69 million shares to the Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation, named after his late wife. He will split 1.845 million shares between his three children's philanthropic ventures: the Sherwood Foundation, the Howard G. Buffett Foundation, and NoVo Foundation.

"My children have given thoughtfully to worthy causes for decades, and I trust 100% both their abilities and their fidelity," the 96-year-old wrote.

This year's donation is more than four times greater than the amount set aside for charity last year. In July, the "Oracle from Omaha" promised to donate more than 99% of his entire Berkshire Hathaway fortune — more than $160 billion — to good causes by 2034 through these foundations.

"Last Thanksgiving I wrote that kindness is costless but also priceless," Buffett wrote in his letter on Monday. "A year later, I would only add that it compounds, and at a better rate than anything Charlie and I ever bought."

In September, Buffett stepped down as chair of Berkshire Hathaway, marking the end of his 50-plus-year tenure. He remains on the board of directors.

In his Thanksgiving letter, he encouraged others to give this holiday season.

"Much of my good fortune has come in the form of people who were kind to me and expected nothing in return. I'm grateful to every one of them," Buffett wrote. "This Thanksgiving, thank one of those selfless people in your own life. Better yet, be a benefactor for someone else."
