Marc Andreessen says government regulation will save jobs from AI — at the cost of economic growth

2026-11-17

Por Jennifer Schonberger

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/marc-andreessen-says-government-regulation-will-save-jobs-from-ai--at-the-cost-of-economic-growth-214918442.html

Venture capitalist Marc Andreessen, co-head of Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh's task force on jobs and productivity, predicted that artificial intelligence will have a dual effect in the job market: Job losses in government-regulated sectors will be insulated from major job losses, but those regulations will limit economic and productivity growth.

"You might interpret this as good news because it means a revolution is not going to destroy all the jobs or kill everybody," he said. "You could also view this as very negative, which is productivity growth just simply may not grow very much. Economic growth just simply may not grow very much."

Andreessen sees two categories of economic sectors: fast-moving sectors that see rapid technological and economic change, along with rapid job turnover — industries like consumer electronics and media entertainment. The other sectors are a combination of law and government, which are not structured to change rapidly and are typically characterized by extensive subsidization.

That means the fastest-growing sectors will see their prices go down, while the slower-growing regulated or government-subsidized sectors will see their prices go up.

"Your iPhone's getting cheaper, the flat-screen TV on your wall is getting cheaper. The bad news is college education is going to cost a million bucks, healthcare is becoming unaffordable, and good luck buying a house," he said.
