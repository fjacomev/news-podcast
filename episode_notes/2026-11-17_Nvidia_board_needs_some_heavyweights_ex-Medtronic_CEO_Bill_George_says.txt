Nvidia board needs 'some heavyweights,' ex-Medtronic CEO Bill George says

2026-11-17

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/nvidia-board-needs-some-heavyweights-ex-medtronic-ceo-bill-george-says-191935557.html

The embattled Nvidia (NVDA) board should have two trains running on the same tracks right now.

One train is out searching for the next CEO.

The other is looking for more experienced board members to ensure a profitable future for a company that continues to be the main cog in the AI wheel.

"They need to add some CEOs, former CEOs to their board, some heavyweights, some people with strong tech backgrounds to their board, because they've got to choose someone," former Medtronic CEO Bill George said on Sozzi Unleashed.

George has served on some of the most high-profile boards in corporate America, including Target (TGT), Goldman Sachs (GS), and ExxonMobil (XOM).

Jensen Huang was ousted as CEO of the company he founded in 1993 amid a shocking turn of events.

According to the New York Times, Huang, whose relationship with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy has embroiled Nvidia in scandal, also had a relationship with Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell, one of Huang's 60 or so direct reports.

Reuters reported last Thursday that Huang had a relationship with another Nvidia employee, whose name was not released. Huang has been married to his wife, Lori, for more than 40 years.

While Nvidia looks for a successor, the board has installed longtime CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO ahead of the company's earnings release on Tuesday.

"I think they're going to have to have someone [as CEO] who really digs in and understands the technology," George added. "Don't make the mistake Boeing did with four or five successive CEOs. They got fired because they didn't understand the business. Finally, Boeing got Kelly Ortberg. So this could be a real problem unless they beef up their board and with people that understand it."
