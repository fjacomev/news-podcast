Disney stock rises after Q4 earnings beat estimates, company sets $3 billion cost savings target

2026-11-18

Por Brooke DiPalma

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/disney-stock-rises-after-q4-earnings-beat-estimates-company-sets-3-billion-cost-savings-target-152507234.html

Disney's (DIS) fourth quarter earnings beat expectations as the media giant's parks and streaming businesses continued to deliver strong growth while the company focused on streamlining operations.

The stock rose 2% in early trading as investors homed in on plans to cut costs by roughly $3 billion by 2029.

"Most of the savings will be used to fund future growth," new CEO Josh D'Amaro told investors on the earnings call. "Now, changes like these are not easy, and they affect people … these steps are important if we want to be a company that moves faster, adapts to a rapidly changing environment, and keeps investing in the content and technology that will drive our growth."

Disney's cost-cutting efforts follow the company's late-September job cuts of approximately 300 roles across human resources and tech. The company said it is using artificial intelligence to accelerate pre- and postproduction processes, design planning around experiences, and operations.

In fiscal Q4, Disney posted adjusted earnings per share of $1.77, above the $1.68 Wall Street expected, alongside revenue of $25.9 billion, which was slightly above the $25.2 billion forecast, according to Bloomberg consensus data.

"Our strong fiscal 2026 results, delivered amid a challenging macroeconomic environment, show we are succeeding on that mission [to be the world's leading entertainment company]," D'Amaro wrote in a shareholder letter.

Total operating income for the company grew 43% year over year to $5 billion, above the expected $4.9 billion, which was the guidance Disney shared last quarter.

For the full year, the company reported revenue of $102.3 billion, alongside adjusted earnings per share of $7.02. Both metrics beat the Street's expectations of $101.5 billion in sales and $6.93 in adjusted earnings per share.

Disney also announced a 33% increase in its dividend to $2 per share, with $1 paid on Jan. 22, 2027, and the rest paid on July 13, 2027.

For 2027, Disney expects segment operating income to grow in the high single digits across Experiences, Entertainment, and Sports. Disney guided for double-digit growth in adjusted earnings per share, assuming "no material deterioration of the global macroeconomic environment."

For the first quarter, Disney expects total segment operating income of approximately $5.4 billion.

Parks and streaming strength drives results

Overall revenue from the Disney Experiences segment, which includes US parks and experiences and the expanding Disney Cruise Line portfolio, came in higher than expected at a record $10.1 billion, above the $9.9 billion forecast.
