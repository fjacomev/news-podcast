Goldman Sachs is looking for a big year for stocks in 2027

2026-11-18

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/goldman-sachs-is-looking-for-a-big-year-for-stocks-in-2027-142151047.html

Goldman Sachs is backing up the truck on stocks for 2027.

"We forecast an S&P 500 total return of 18% to a year-end 2027 level of 8,700, including a dividend yield of 1%," Goldman Sachs strategist Ben Snider wrote on Tuesday.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC) closed on Monday at 7,416.59 and is within arm's reach of double-digit gains for 2026.

Snider added, "Earnings should grow by a robust 11% in 2027, or 18% excluding mega-cap tech "other income" from private investment stakes. However, that is a step down from the roughly 30% growth rate in 2026, and growth will likely slow further in 2028."
