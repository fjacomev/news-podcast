Home Depot taps Richard McPhail as new CEO, reports Q3 earnings beat

2026-11-18

Por Brooke DiPalma

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/home-depot-taps-richard-mcphail-as-new-ceo-reports-q3-earnings-beat-111428412.html

Home Depot (HD) beat Wall Street's metrics across the board in the third quarter and adjusted its full-year outlook amid a shake-up in its C-suite leadership.

The company announced that Richard McPhail, who joined the company in 2005 and currently serves as CFO, will become CEO on Nov. 23. Current CEO Ted Decker has returned from temporary medical leave and will continue to serve as chair of the board, Home Depot said.

Decker, who became CEO in 2022, told investors on its earnings call that he is "grateful for the support" he received, and that "The Board and I have agreed that now is the right time to move to the next phase of our succession plan."

Jordan Broggi, a 13-year veteran of Home Depot, will take the CFO role, while senior executive vice president Ann-Marie Campbell was promoted to president and COO.

In the third quarter, revenue grew by nearly 5% to $43.4 billion, above the $42.8 billion analysts forecast, per Bloomberg estimates. Adjusted earnings per share grew 3% to $3.86, more than the $3.85 predicted.

"Results in the third quarter exceeded our expectations," incoming CEO McPhail said in the release. "Our associates' strong engagement and exceptional execution, combined with our continued investments across the business, enabled us to grow market share in a dynamic environment."

Home Depot stock declined slightly in premarket trading following the announcement.

Same-store sales increased 2.2%, more than the roughly 1.7% expected. US same-store sales also jumped 2.2%, above the 1.5% expected, but a sluggish housing environment persists, as US homebuilder confidence fell to its lowest level in more than 14 years.

The company said the average ticket size increased by 3% due to a mix of inflation and some customers trading up to new and innovative products. But customers had fewer visits to Home Depot stores.

Transactions for big ticket items above $1,000 increased by 4.5% compared to Q3 last year.

Home Depot said it saw strength in its power department, which saw double-digit growth, as well as in storage, plumbing, and electrical, which grew by mid-single-digit percentages.

These results come as the average 30-year fixed-rate remains above 7%.

The company adjusted its 2026 forecast. It now expects total sales to grow by 4.5%, at the high end of its previously expected range for the year, and expects its same-store sales growth outlook to be up 1.4%, toward the higher end of its previously expected range of flat to up 2%.

McPhail said that "a rising interest rate environment puts pressure on our customer and our business" and its guidance reflects that.

Lowe's (LOW) is expected to post a same-store sales increase of 0.4% when it reports quarterly results on Wednesday.
