Treasuries Rise as Risk-Averse Investors Dump Stocks, Buy Bonds

2026-11-18

Por Elizabeth Stanton; Victor Swezey

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/articles/treasuries-rise-risk-averse-investors-162624439.html

(Bloomberg) -- Treasuries gained as investors, alarmed by the latest in a series of technology breakdowns over the past week, flocked to US government bonds as a haven from stocks.

The rally trimmed yields across maturities by as much as four to five basis points. They had risen earlier in the session after October retail sales data were stronger than anticipated, backing expectations for additional Federal Reserve interest-rate hikes.

The bond rally coincided with US equity market benchmarks tumbling to session lows, led by large technology company shares. The S&P 500 Index fell as much as 1.7%, the Nasdaq 100 Index 2.3% to the lowest level since April, in the minutes after CNN reported that a National Security Agency network had gone down, the latest in a series of breakdowns that have crippled airlines and financial services companies over the past week.

"Fear of the unknown seems to be dominating rate space near term," said David Robin, an interest-rate strategist at TJM Institutional Services LLC. "The bursting of the AI bubble, coupled with the difficult-to-quantify-or-understand headline risk of technology breakdowns and cyber attacks, is causing a flight to safety."

It's aggravated, Robin said, by uncertainty regarding what the Fed will do on Dec. 9 and the paucity of influential economic data in the next two weeks. Short-term interest-rate contracts fully price in a quarter-point interest rate hike as well as an outside chance of a half-point increase.

"There really wasn't anything to explain the hit to equities and the bid to Treasuries beyond the NSA headlines," said Izaac Brook, US rates strategist at RBC Capital Markets. "This is all just catalyst for chopping around in the range we carved out after payrolls, inflation data and Fedspeak."
