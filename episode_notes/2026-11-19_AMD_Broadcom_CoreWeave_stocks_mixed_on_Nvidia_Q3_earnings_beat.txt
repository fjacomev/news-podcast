AMD, Broadcom, CoreWeave stocks mixed on Nvidia Q3 earnings beat

2026-11-19

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/amd-broadcom-tsmc-stocks-rise-on-nvidia-earnings-coreweave-nebius-shares-also-rally-220159436.html

Semiconductor and cloud provider stocks were mixed on Wednesday after AI chip giant Nvidia (NVDA) reported Q3 results that beat Wall Street's expectations. Peers AMD (AMD) and Broadcom (AVGO) shares rose more than 3%. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSM), which manufactures Nvidia's chips, also saw its stock rise modestly.

The semiconductor trade has been volatile since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO last Thursday following reports of extramarital affairs. Wall Street's questions over a succession plan have kept shares of the AI chip heavyweight and the AI trade under pressure.

"I hope that this helps to calm some jitters in the market about the transition," Brian Colello, Morningstar analyst, told Deirdre Bosa Live on Tuesday.

"Seems like business as usual and the forecast is really encouraging as well," he added.

Other areas of the chip trade wavered, including memory chipmaker Micron Technology (MU) and SK Hynix (SKHY), which fell modestly.

Meanwhile, Nvidia-backed CoreWeave (CRWV) fell 4%. Nvidia owns more than 10% of CoreWeave and is a major chip supplier to the AI infrastructure provider. Peer Nebius (NBIS) rose 1%.
