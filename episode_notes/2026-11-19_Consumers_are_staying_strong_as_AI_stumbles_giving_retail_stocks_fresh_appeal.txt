Consumers are staying strong as AI stumbles — giving retail stocks fresh appeal

2026-11-19

Por Hamza Shaban

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/consumers-are-staying-strong-as-ai-stumbles--giving-retail-stocks-fresh-appeal-110000718.html

Americans' capacity to spend continues to buoy the economy despite a cost-of-living crunch. The latest measures of consumer retail spending continue a years-long story about cash-strapped households soldering through inflation — and doing so especially as other key parts of the economy look weaker.

As the AI robots stumble, the flesh-and-blood American consumer is still out there on two feet and spending, even if they are looking for deals.

This week's corporate earnings so far show that even though there are bright spots heading into the holiday shopping season, there are still signs of stress and a renewed focus on seeking value above all else.

US retail sales slowed from September but still increased slightly in October, as households pulled back on large purchases like cars and furniture while keeping up spending elsewhere ahead of the holiday shopping season.

Focusing on the underlying strength and resilience of the consumer is easier to do after Target (TGT) posted a standout quarter. The retailer reported a 5% increase in foot traffic, the highest year-over-year jump since January 2022. Net sales jumped slightly more than 7%, with beauty products growing by greater than 9% and food and beverage rising roughly 7%.

Despite inflation and high gas prices, consumers are still swiping and tapping their way through it.

In an interview with Yahoo Finance, Target CEO Michael Fiddelke attributed the company's turnaround success to its fixation on value, an especially potent proposition for consumers in this economy.

"The 'I can't believe the quality of this' or 'I can't believe I found it at Target at a price point' that makes the smile get wider. Once someone turns over the price tag, that's when we're at our best," Fiddelke said.

Inflation's squeeze doesn't always reduce overall spending. It can steer dollars in different directions, influencing how people spend their money.

Home Depot (HD), which posted an earnings beat on Wednesday, said its average ticket size increased by 3% from a mix of higher prices and customers trading up to new and innovative products. But even though transactions were slightly bigger, customers came into the store less often.

"We expect HD will explore price increases in certain categories and lean into cost savings opportunities to navigate this challenging supply chain and broader cost backdrop," Bank of America analysts led by Christopher Nardone wrote in a note earlier this week.

The higher cost of gas and airfare may also juice holiday shopping in a roundabout way.
