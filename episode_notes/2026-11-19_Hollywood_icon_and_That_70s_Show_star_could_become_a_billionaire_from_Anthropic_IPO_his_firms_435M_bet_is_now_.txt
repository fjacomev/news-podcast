Ashton Kutcher

could be on his way to billionaire status.

The 48-year-old actor stands to be one of the biggest winners as Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude assistant, prepares to go public at a proposed $1.4 trillion valuation, according to

Page Six

. Sound Ventures, the venture capital firm Kutcher co-founded, has invested about $435 million in Anthropic, and that stake is now reportedly worth about $7 billion, per

Newsweek

Ashton Kutcher’s Anthropic Investment

Sound Ventures first backed Anthropic in 2023, after Kutcher and his business partner Guy Oseary met with the company’s CEO, Dario Amodei,

Bloomberg

reported. The firm kept investing in later rounds and now owns roughly 0.5% of the company.

Kutcher won’t walk away with a $7 billion check, though. Sound Ventures manages money for outside investors, so any profits will be split among several parties.

Ashton Kutcher’s History as a Tech Investor

Kutcher has quietly become one of Hollywood’s most successful tech investors, with a reported net worth of more than $200 million. In 2010, he co-founded A-Grade Investments with billionaire Ron Burkle and Oseary, the longtime manager of

Madonna

and the Red Hot Chili Peppers. Their early bets included Spotify, Uber and Airbnb, and they reportedly grew $30 million into about $250 million, per

Newsweek

Kutcher and Oseary launched Sound Ventures in 2015 as A-Grade’s successor. In July, Kutcher stepped down as a partner but stayed on as a senior advisor.

The Wall Street Journal

reported the decision was partly over differing views on which startups to back: the firm wanted more established companies, while Kutcher prefers early-stage ones. He has since launched a new fund with Morgan Beller, a former co-leader of Meta’s

Libra

project.
