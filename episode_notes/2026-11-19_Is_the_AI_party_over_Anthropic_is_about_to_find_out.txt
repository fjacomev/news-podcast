Thursday is supposed to be a celebration of AI.

Anthropic (ANT) is heading toward its long-awaited IPO at the Nasdaq, potentially giving Wall Street one of the biggest new pure-play AI investment stories in years.

Before you hit me up on X, Elon Musk-led SpaceX (SPCX) is not a pure-play AI company. It's an industrial company with a whole lot of AI bolted on.

But there's a problem amid all the Anthropic IPO hoopla.

The very stocks that fueled this AI boom are still under pressure. Just look at the sell-offs this week in top tech names like CoreWeave (CRWV), Salesforce (CRM), and even AMD (AMD).

Investors are questioning the AI spending, the valuations, the financing arrangements — and whether the returns will ever justify the billions being poured into this technology.

And the timing couldn't be more fascinating.

Truist chief market strategist Keith Lerner noted that tech valuations have fallen 42% over the past year. The sector has gone from outperforming the S&P 500 (^GSPC) by 25% over six months to underperforming it by 6%. Bank of America's latest fund manager survey shows investors have cut tech exposure by the most since June 2006.

That's a serious reset.

The question pre-Anthropic IPO: Is Wall Street finally getting too negative?

The AI build-out isn't disappearing, as we saw from Nvidia (NVDA) on Tuesday. Companies are still spending. The technology is still evolving. And as Lerner pointed out, previous tech pullbacks of more than 10% during this bull market were followed by an average rebound of 47%.

That doesn't guarantee a repeat. There are real risks — and this AI stock sell-off could get worse.

Anthropic's IPO arrives at a moment when investors are suddenly asking what could go wrong with AI.

Maybe it's time to ask the other question: What could go right?

Investing in Anthropic is like investing in a young Amazon (AMZN), "Shark Tank" star Kevin O'Leary told me on Sozzi Unleashed. Valid point.

Indeed, the biggest opportunity in tech may emerge when Wall Street least wants to own it.

That's the trade I'm watching. Well, that and the first few ticks in ticker symbol ANT!
