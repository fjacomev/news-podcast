T.J. Maxx owner's secret weapon: Catering to last-minute gift buyers

2026-11-19

Por Hamza Shaban

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/tj-maxx-owners-secret-weapon-catering-to-last-minute-gift-buyers-110000933.html

It's an unwelcome but familiar feeling: You've run out to the store the week of Christmas to buy the gifts you neglected to get during the entire month of December. But now the toy aisles are bare, nothing on the racks is in the right size, and Amazon can't deliver the gadget your nephew asked you for until after the holiday.

Don't fret. TJX (TJX), the parent company of T.J. Maxx, Marshalls, and HomeGoods, has planned for this. They know you're a late gift buyer. They've got you covered.

CEO Ernie Herrman said part of the company's success in recent years is its fresh inventory, which is updated later into December than at other brick-and-mortar retailers.

"We create an opportunity for a consumer that doesn't want to go online and wants something fresh when a lot of stores are picked over," Herrman said on the company's earnings call Wednesday. "They can come into TJ Maxx or Marshalls and find something there that is just landing 10 days before Christmas, which I think is really groundbreaking in terms of the approach from what other retailers do."

TJX stock surged 9% after posting impressive quarterly results, exceeding expectations and upgrading its full-year outlook on expectations of strong holiday demand for its discount shops.
