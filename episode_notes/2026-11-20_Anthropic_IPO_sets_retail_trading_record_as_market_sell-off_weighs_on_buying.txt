Anthropic IPO sets retail trading record as market sell-off weighs on buying

2026-11-20

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/retail-traders-pile-into-anthropic-ipo-but-net-buying-lags-spacex-212350846.html

Anthropic's (ANT) stock churned out more retail trading activity on its public debut than any other stock in its listing day going back to 2012, according to full Thursday trading data from Vanda Research.

The firm's data shows everyday investors traded $772 million worth of Anthropic shares, exceeding the $669 million recorded during SpaceX's (SPCX) first trading session in June and setting a record in Vanda's data, which goes back to 2012.

However, everyday investors were far less aggressive net buyers of shares through the day compared to SpaceX's blockbuster June listing. Net retail purchases totaled just $60 million or roughly half the $118 million that retail investors poured into SpaceX on its first day and a third less than the $92 million in Coinbase Global's (COIN) April 2021 debut, according to Vanda.

"Retail are turning up to trade Anthropic ... but not in the way that much of the street, ourselves included, had probably anticipated," Vanda Research analyst Viraj Patel wrote, noting that the share of retail buyers steadily declined across early trading.

Among the major reasons for the lower net buying, Patel cited "broader risk aversion in global markets" as major stock indexes fell Thursday. Smaller investors also may appear to be favoring an alternative to traditional stock trading, specifically the use of a crypto-related derivative product known as perpetual futures, which are tied to the price of the stock, Patel added.

The divergence suggests that retail investors were actively buying and selling Anthropic shares rather than accumulating them as aggressively as they did SpaceX. The net buying was also far below Vanda's estimate made ahead of the listing based on perpetual futures pricing.

The broader selloff came as retail investors have sold single stocks to fund their Anthropic purchases, rotating out of AI names like AMD (AMD) as buying in Nvidia (NVDA) stalled. Shares of AMD and Nvidia dropped 7% and 2%, respectively, in afternoon trading.

The final word on retail enthusiasm for the stock is still far from out, according to Patel. SpaceX's first-day net buying accounted for just 29% of its total in the first week, according to the company.
