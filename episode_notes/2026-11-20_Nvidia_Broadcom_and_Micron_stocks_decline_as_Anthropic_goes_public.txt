Nvidia, Broadcom, and Micron stocks decline as Anthropic goes public

2026-11-20

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-broadcom-micron-stocks-decline-as-anthropic-goes-public-170845481.html

Chip giant Nvidia (NVDA) stock slid into the red on Thursday, along with Broadcom (AVGO) shares, after Anthropic began trading. Intel (INTC) stock pared gains, closing up more than 1%.

Meanwhile, shares of chipmaker AMD (AMD) sank 7% after Hunterbrook Media disclosed a short position in the stock, citing questions about whether the company's semiconductors continued to reach a US-blacklisted Chinese server maker through intermediary companies despite export restrictions.

Memory chipmaker Micron Technology (MU) also lost gains as did peer SK Hynix (SKHY), dropping more than 2%.

Micron president and chief technology officer Scott DeBoer reiterated on Deirdre Bosa Live on Wednesday that memory supply in 2027 will be tighter than in 2026.

"Our supply, we don't see it coming into balance with demand really in any time we can actually pick in the future right now," he said.

The broader chip sector fell on Thursday following recent volatility, as the exit of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and concerns over AI financing have created jitters across the AI trade.

Shares of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSM) and equipment maker ASML (ASML) erased earlier gains Thursday, leading the Technology (XLK) sector lower.

Other areas of the AI trade also trimmed gains on Thursday, with optical equipment maker Coherent (COHR) trimming gains. Bloom Energy (BE) shares declined modestly. Neocloud providers CoreWeave (CRWV) and Nebius (NBIS) both erased gains to close down 6% and 9% respectively for the session.

"Anthropic's IPO is a big near-term win for the tech sector and should help restore some investor confidence that the AI Revolution growth thesis is not waning," Yorkville Ives' Dan Ives said on Wednesday evening.

The analyst noted that the AI trade is going through "a very turbulent, white-knuckle period that does not change our longer-term view that $3 trillion to $4 trillion of capex will be spent over the next few years."

The frontier lab's IPO "sets the stage for the next phase of tech and infrastructure investment, with Anthropic a central player in the buildout," Ives added.
