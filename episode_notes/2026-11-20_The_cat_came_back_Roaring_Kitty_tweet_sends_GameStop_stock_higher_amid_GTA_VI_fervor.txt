The cat came back: Roaring Kitty tweet sends GameStop stock higher amid GTA VI fervor

2026-11-20

Por Paige Oamek

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/the-cat-came-back-roaring-kitty-tweet-sends-gamestop-stock-higher-amid-gta-vi-fervor-184220345.html

Influential investor Keith Gill, also known as Roaring Kitty, has returned, and GameStop (GME) shares are rising after the meme stock trader who made millions betting on the company returned to the discourse as gamers everywhere celebrated the release of "Grand Theft Auto VI."

After being MIA online for nearly two years, at 11:30 a.m. ET on Thursday, Roaring Kitty tested the waters with a mysterious post on X: a photo of "Cat Mario."

GameStop stock popped roughly 3% immediately following his tweet.

Gill's post lands at a dizzying moment for markets, just roughly an hour after Anthropic's (ANT) IPO went live and on the day Take-Two (TTWO) launched GTA VI.

Yesterday, GameStop CEO Ryan Cohen said publishers are making a mistake by releasing games as digital downloads instead of physical discs. "When you buy a game, you should be able to sell it, trade it or lend it to a friend," Cohen told Bloomberg.

GameStop's stock is up more than 30% since the beginning of the year, buoyed by its eBay (EBAY) stake and interest.

The image is of Mario wearing a cat power-up in "Super Mario 3D World." It's not clear what Roaring Kitty's Mario post means — if anything at all — but the market is reacting and powering up the stock.
