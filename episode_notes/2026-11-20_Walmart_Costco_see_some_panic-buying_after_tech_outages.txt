Walmart, Costco see some panic-buying after tech outages

2026-11-20

Por Brooke DiPalma

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/walmart-costco-see-some-panic-buying-after-tech-outages-191922568.html

A wave of tech outages and disruptions affecting Apple (AAPL), Chase (JPM), Microsoft (MSFT), and US airlines has sent consumers spiraling over the past week, prompting people to stock up on emergency supplies.

"We have seen a small amount of that," Walmart (WMT) CFO John David Rainey told Yahoo Finance (see video above). "Certainly categories like jug bottled water or propane are selling at higher comps with that concern, but it's really relatively small to our overall business. It's worth noting that oftentimes when we see that, it's a pull forward of future demand."

Rainey said the company will stick to its outlook despite a minimal sales lift. On Thursday, Walmart raised its full-year outlook for the third time this year. It now expects net sales to increase by 4.9% to 5.2%, up from 4% to 5%.

Walmart isn't alone in seeing panic buying amid consumer anxieties that a cyberattack or other outage could lead to more catastrophic system failures. Wholesale retailer Costco (COST) is also seeing a boost, according to Mizuho analyst David Bellinger.

"Consumer anxiety, following the Microsoft Outlook outage, the ground stoppage for all U.S. airline flights, and apocalyptic concerns flaring up across social media, have all contributed to increased emergency prep sales at Costco," he wrote in a note to clients.

As of Nov. 15, he said 21 of the 26 high-ticket generators online were sold out.

"These one-time lifts, combined with pantry-loading and a value connotation into the holiday period, all add to an already very elevated valuation for shares," Bellinger said, who raised his price target on Costco stock to $1,150 from $1,110.

Shoppers stockpiling emergency supplies led to an increase in same-store sales growth at US Costco stores, not including fuel purchases. Sales increased 11% to 12% in the final week of October, Bellinger wrote, adding that the bump continued in the first two weeks of November.

Search "doomsday food" on the Costco app, and shoppers will find that emergency food supplies from ReadyWise, Ready Project, and Chef's Banquet are all sold out.

Yahoo Finance also reached out to Target (TGT) about whether it's seeing any panic buying, but the retailer said it has nothing to share at the moment.
