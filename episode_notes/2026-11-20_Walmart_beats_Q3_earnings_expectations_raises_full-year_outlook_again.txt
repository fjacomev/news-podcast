Walmart beats Q3 earnings expectations, raises full-year outlook again

2026-11-20

Por Brooke DiPalma

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/walmart-beats-q3-earnings-expectations-raises-full-year-outlook-again-184415236.html

Walmart's (WMT) third quarter earnings came in ahead of expectations, and the retail giant raised its outlook for fiscal 2027 as e-commerce sales surged.

America's biggest retailer, often considered a bellwether for the industry and a key pulse check for what the holiday season could look like, posted revenue of $188.8 billion, just above the $187.2 billion expected, according to Bloomberg consensus data. Adjusted earnings per share clocked in at $0.65, a cent higher than expectations.

US same-store sales growth moderated to 2.9% year over year, as analysts forecast. E-commerce sales grew 17% globally and 23% in the US.

CEO John Furner said the company "delivered strong results" and its core business is "gaining market share." He added that growth is coming from several parts of Walmart's business model, including "membership and agentic shopping."

Walmart raised its 2026 outlook for the third time this year. It now expects net sales to increase by 4.9% to 5.2%, up from 4% to 5%.

Operating income is expected to be in the 8% to 8.6% range, above the previously expected 7% to 8.5% range. Adjusted earnings outlook is also expected to be in the range of $2.84 to $2.89, above the previous range of $2.80 to $2.87.

Furner told investors on the call that Walmart can now reach 96% of US households in less than 3 hours, and 60% in less than 30 minutes.

"We have a unique ability to get closer to customers, use inventory more effectively, and deliver with speed at a low relative cost. Over 80% of e-commerce orders in the US are fulfilled from stores, including our marketplace orders," he added.

During the quarter, Walmart's US advertising business grew 37%. The company also introduced a subscription bundle that combines Walmart+ and Sam's Club Plus for $120 per year, plus tax.

Sam's Club's same-store sales grew 9.7% as Americans turned to the wholesale retailer for fuel, grocery items, and general merchandise.

Walmart US CEO David Guggina said that the Walmart+ membership base grew in the double digits following the launch of its bundle option, and members increased their online spending with Walmart by more than 80% compared to before the launch. He added that customers with a membership spend four times as much as non-members.

Grocery sales also increased in the mid-single digits in the quarter as shoppers focused on essentials while also seeking out holiday items. The company posted some of the highest gains in groceries in three years.

Walmart cut prices on almost 8,000 products at its stores in the quarter. Furner said the company is doing all it can to keep prices as low as possible, despite pressure from higher fuel costs.
