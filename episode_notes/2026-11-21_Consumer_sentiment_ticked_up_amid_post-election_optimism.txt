Consumer sentiment ticked up amid post-election optimism

2026-11-21

Por Claire Boston

https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/article/consumer-sentiment-ticked-up-amid-post-election-optimism-152954161.html

Consumer sentiment ticked higher in November as consumers grew more confident about short-term and long-term business conditions following the election.

The University of Michigan's Consumer Sentiment Index rose 7.3% to 51.5 in November, from October's reading of 48. Economists expected a similarly sized improvement to 51.6.

There's evidence that consumers' economic views have shifted after Democrats' massive gains in the Nov. 6 midterm elections. November's survey was conducted between Oct. 20 and Nov. 16, and expectations surged among independent voters, a group that largely moved left this cycle. Expectations also improved for Democrats, and fell among Republicans.

"Consumers appear to anticipate potential changes to public policy under a divided government to come," Joanne Hsu, the survey's director, said in a statement.

Lower-wealth consumers saw their personal finances improve the most last month, helped by lower gas prices. But those with the most stock holdings said their personal finances deteriorated, likely due to recent market fluctuations stemming from AI jitters.

Americans' inflation expectations for the year ahead improved markedly this month, falling to 3.7% from 4.7% in October. Still, the numbers remain higher than the 3.4% consumers expected in February, before the Iran War began.

While the numbers improved this month, elevated inflation, high gas prices, and the potential for an artificial intelligence bubble are still leaving consumers feeling gloomy about the economy.

Claire Boston is a senior reporter for Yahoo Finance covering housing, mortgages, and home insurance.
