Gap stock pops even as warm fall weather leads the retail giant to cut its forecast

2026-11-21

Por Brian Sozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/gap-stock-pops-even-as-warm-fall-weather-leads-the-retail-giant-to-cut-its-forecast-130937620.html

The warm fall weather hasn't been kind to Gap (GAP). But investors don't seem to care.

Shares of the owner of Gap, Old Navy, Banana Republic, and Athleta rose 15% as investors shrugged off a mixed quarter and focused on improved sales at Old Navy and continued strength at Gap.

Third-quarter sales rose 2% to $4 billion, while comparable sales were flat. Adjusted earnings came in at $0.82 a share, topping estimates of $0.78.

Comparable sales at the Gap brand jumped 9%, marking 12 straight quarters of gains.

Old Navy comparable sales were flat, improving from a 4% drop in the second quarter as the company course-corrected on its assortments.

Banana Republic fell 3%, and Athleta continued to be a challenge, as seen in a 15% sales drop.

But warmer fall weather softened demand in October and early November, forcing Gap to slash its full-year sales outlook to roughly flat from prior guidance for 1% to 1.5% growth.

The company also cut its adjusted earnings forecast to $2.29 to $2.34 a share from $2.35 to $2.45.

"We view 3Q as mixed, with Gap Inc. delivering better-than-expected profitability and sequential improvement at Old Navy, while Gap brand momentum remained strong. However, softer October/early November demand drove lower FY26 sales and profit guidance, with added promotions weighing on 4Q GM. We see encouraging underlying progress, but holiday execution remains key," Jefferies analyst Corey Tarlowe said.
