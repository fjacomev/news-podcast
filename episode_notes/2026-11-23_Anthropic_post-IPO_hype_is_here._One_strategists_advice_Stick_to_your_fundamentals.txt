Stick to your investing discipline as the post-IPO hype begins for newly minted public company Anthropic (ANT).

"I do think this name is very hyped. Whether that's deserved or not is definitely debatable," Renaissance Capital director of investment strategies Avery Marquez said on Sozzi Unleashed. "But don't go into it just with FOMO [fear of missing out] or hype. You really need to take a measured approach and stick to your fundamentals."

Shares of the AI model builder dropped in early trading on Friday as investors booked profits after a frenetic first day of trading. Anthropic made a monster debut on Wall Street Thursday, with shares opening at $111.50 a little before noon ET, a 34% premium to the $83 IPO price.

But the AI company's stock couldn't hold on to its early gains and closed at $97.17, up 17% on the day. That closing price gave Anthropic a market capitalization of about $1.28 trillion, or roughly $1.75 trillion on a fully diluted basis.

To Marquez's point, there may be a near-term information vacuum about Anthropic, set against ongoing hype. For one, Wall Street analyst coverage of Anthropic won't begin for a few weeks. And two, the company's first earnings report as a public company is months away.

That leaves Anthropic's interesting S-1 as the continued source of fundamental truth on the company.

In a recent amended S-1 filing, Anthropic said that third quarter sales rose 1,127% to $17 billion. Revenue growth slowed on a quarter-over-quarter basis. Third quarter sales increased 47% compared with a 144% growth rate in the second quarter. Sales increased 132% in the first quarter.

The company also lost a staggering $23.29 billion in the third quarter.
