Congress closes in on Chinese car ban, with a possible way out for Mercedes

2026-11-23

Por Pras Subramanian

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/congress-closes-in-on-chinese-car-ban-with-a-possible-way-out-for-mercedes-160342747.html

US lawmakers are moving to push a ban on Chinese cars into law, and Mercedes-Benz (MBG.DE) may get a way out.

Congress is close to a deal in the lame-duck session on legislation that would effectively keep Chinese-made cars out of the US, per Bloomberg. Backers, many from states with big auto plants, want the bill on President Trump's desk before January.

The deal could also loosen earlier proposed restrictions that would ban car sales by automakers with Chinese ownership stakes. That group includes Mercedes, which has Chinese shareholders holding approximately 20% of its stock. Per the report, the rules under discussion would allow sales if those Chinese stakes are passive, meaning they carry no voting power.

Chinese cars are already mostly kept out of the US. A 100% tariff on Chinese-made EVs effectively blocks them, and Commerce Department rules on Chinese connected-vehicle technology bar Chinese-linked automakers from selling connected cars in the US starting with the 2027 model year. A law would make that ban far harder to undo.

The earlier version of the bill put Mercedes at risk. The bill from Sens. Bernie Moreno (R-Ohio) and Elissa Slotkin (D-Mich.), which cleared a Senate committee earlier this year, would bar connected-car sales by any automaker with Chinese ownership above 15%.

China's state-owned BAIC Group holds 9.98% of Mercedes' voting rights. Geely founder Li Shufu holds 9.69% through his Tenaciou3 Prospect Investment Limited. Together, that puts Mercedes over the threshold.

Moreno walked industry representatives through the revised language on Wednesday, Bloomberg reported, and it includes protections that would keep Mercedes from being banned outright.

Sen. Katie Britt (R-Ala.), whose state builds Mercedes SUVs, told Bloomberg she expects both the automaker and Alabama to come out protected. "We understand that there will be some adjustments that have to be made," she said.

Yahoo Finance reached out to Mercedes for comment but did not immediately hear back. The company told Bloomberg that it continues "to meet with all stakeholders who have a shared interest."

Mercedes has previously told Yahoo Finance that no shareholder holds more than 10% of its stock, and that its major shareholders have no "control or decision-making authority" over the company. The automaker said it is directly and indirectly responsible for 160,000 US jobs, with plants in Alabama and South Carolina.

Recall that Polestar (PSNY), which is controlled by China's Geely, ceased sales in the US after it did not receive a waiver from the Commerce Department to sell its vehicles in the country, even though its Polestar 3 is made in the US.
