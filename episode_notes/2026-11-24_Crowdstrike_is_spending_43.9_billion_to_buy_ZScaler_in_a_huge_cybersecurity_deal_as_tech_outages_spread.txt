CrowdStrike is spending $43.9 billion to buy Zscaler in a huge cybersecurity deal as tech outages spread

With tech outages ripping across the world, CrowdStrike (CRWD) is shelling out big-time money to acquire fellow cybersecurity player and longtime partner Zscaler (ZS).

The two will also combine businesses led by two veteran tech founders: George Kurtz at CrowdStrike and Jay Chaudhry at Zscaler.

CrowdStrike will spend $43.9 billion to buy Zscaler, it said on Monday. The company's market cap stood at $31.7 billion at Friday's close. Chaudhry will continue to lead Zscaler while also joining the board of CrowdStrike.

Shares of CrowdStrike fell 8%, while Zscaler rose 17%.

CrowdStrike said it expects revenue synergies to build progressively over the first 30 months following an expected close in mid 2027, reaching about $1 billion in annualized run-rate revenue synergies by the end of that period. It sees $500 million in cost synergies within 18-24 months of the deal closing.

"We view this as the clearest signal yet that cybersecurity is consolidating into a handful of platforms, with the AI era forcing the hand of vendors who had been content to partner," Yorkville Ives partner Dan Ives said.
