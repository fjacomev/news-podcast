Elon Musk on his support for Jensen Huang: 'Fair-weather friends are worthless'

2026-11-24

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/musk-on-support-for-jensen-huangs-return-to-nvidia-fair-weather-friends-are-worthless-212613771.html

Tesla (TSLA) and SpaceX (SPCX) CEO Elon Musk once again came out in support of his friend Jensen Huang on Monday after Nvidia's board unanimously decided to reinstate him as CEO. Musk also took a swipe at those who may not have stood by Huang as publicly or forcefully as he did.

"Everyone is your friend when the chips are up, but those who support you when the chips are down are your real friends," Musk wrote on X on Monday in response to a post about his support for Huang.

"Fair-weather friends are worthless," Musk added.

Earlier on Monday, Musk immediately responded to the news that Nvidia's board had reinstated the founder as CEO, writing, "It is done." Later, he added, "The mistake has been corrected."

Musk was one of Huang's biggest supporters throughout the ordeal that led to his resignation. He was also vocal about a campaign to bring Huang back as CEO, citing a circulating petition.

Huang abruptly stepped down following reports of alleged affairs with a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed and with at least one Nvidia employee who reported directly to him.

He later said he would return if it was in the best interest of shareholders. The board initially ruled that out, but President Trump said Monday that he personally intervened to help bring him back.

"Jensen drove supply chain brilliantly at Nvidia, but many of those deals were a handshake with Jensen, not a written contract. Without Jensen, at least some of those critical deals will be lost," Musk said after Huang resigned.

Nvidia shares soared 31% following Huang's return as CEO. Tesla and SpaceX stocks also rose amid a broader market rally.
