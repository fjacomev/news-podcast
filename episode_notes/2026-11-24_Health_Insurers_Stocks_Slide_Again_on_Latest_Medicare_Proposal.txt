Health Insurers Stocks Slide Again on Latest Medicare Proposal

2026-11-24

Por John Tozzi

https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/health-insurers-stocks-slide-again-143658768.html

(Bloomberg) -- Shares of US health insurers including UnitedHealth Group Inc. fell sharply in early trading Monday after federal health officials proposed changes to the Medicare program poised to cut billions in payments to insurers in the years ahead.

The rule published late Friday by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services would overhaul the bonus system that rewards Medicare Advantage insurers for high marks on the program's quality ratings, known as stars. The changes, including removing many measures that go into the ratings, would reduce the payments by about $107 billion between 2031 and 2037, the agency estimated.

UnitedHealth shares sank 5.2% at 9:32 a.m. in New York. Shares of CVS Health Corp. fell 2.7% and Humana Inc. dropped 3.4%.

The rule still needs to be finalized and withstand potential legal challenges. It's the latest blow to health insurers' stock prices from policy risks. Last week a separate proposal from CMS to toughen audit rules for Medicare Advantage plans sent insurers' shares down. A fresh proposal from Vermont Senator Bernie Sanders to expand the traditional Medicare program for seniors to younger Americans deepened the slide.

The latest proposal from CMS suggests heightened risks for insurers coming from Washington. The agency "is signaling that the storm of uncertainty is likely to continue," Veda Partners analyst Spencer Perlman wrote in a note Monday.
