Tesla and SpaceX assemble deal teams for a merger that could give Musk majority control

2026-11-24

Por Rani Molla

https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/tesla-and-spacex-assemble-deal-teams-for-a-merger-that-could-give-musk-majority-control-144555932.html

Tesla (TSLA) and SpaceX (SPCX) have enlisted five directors and three investment banks to help move forward what could be the biggest merger in history — and one that could give Elon Musk greater voting power than he currently has at Tesla.

The companies have tapped two directors from SpaceX and three from Tesla — Roelof Botha and Randy Glein from SpaceX, and Robyn Denholm, Kathleen Wilson-Thompson, and Jack Hartung from Tesla — to serve on two special committees overseeing the potential $3.5 trillion merger, the Financial Times reported.

Although all five have longstanding ties to Elon Musk, the CEO of both companies, they were deemed financially "disinterested" parties.

The committees have enlisted Morgan Stanley (MS) and Goldman Sachs (GS) for SpaceX and Evercore (EVR) for Tesla to develop financial models for the proposed all-stock merger. The exchange ratio — which will determine how much of the combined company existing Tesla and SpaceX shareholders will own — has yet to be agreed upon.

The deal, which could be reached before year's end, could give Musk something he's long sought: greater voting power than he currently has at Tesla. That could give Musk more latitude to pursue his ambitions in AI and robotics while diminishing other shareholders' ability to challenge his decisions.

Musk controls about 20% of Tesla's voting power, compared with 84% at SpaceX, where he holds supervoting shares. Depending on the terms of the deal and the governance structure of the combined company, Musk could end up with majority voting control.
