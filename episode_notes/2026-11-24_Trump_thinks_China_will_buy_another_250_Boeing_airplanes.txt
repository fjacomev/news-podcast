Trump thinks China will buy another 250 Boeing airplanes

2026-11-24

Por Reuters

WASHINGTON, Nov 23 (Reuters) - President Donald Trump said Monday he thinks China will agree to buy another 250 Boeing airplanes just days after he met with Chinese leader Xi Jinping.

"I think they're going to do another 250, so I'm going to keep selling," Trump said on CNBC. China in May agreed to buy 200 Boeing airplanes, a number far fewer than analysts had expected and at the time Trump said China could potentially boost its Boeing order to as many as 750 planes.

(Reporting by David Shepardson)
