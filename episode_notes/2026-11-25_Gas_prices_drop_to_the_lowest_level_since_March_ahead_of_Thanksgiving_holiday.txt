Gas prices drop to the lowest level since March ahead of Thanksgiving holiday

2026-11-25

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/gas-prices-drop-to-the-lowest-level-since-march-ahead-of-thanksgiving-holiday-170804061.html

Gasoline prices (RB=F) hovered at their lowest level since March ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday.

The price at the pump fell below $4 per gallon earlier this month and has continued to decline as oil prices hover near three-month lows. The current national average of $3.61 per gallon is $0.68 lower than a month ago, though $0.54 higher than a year ago, according to AAA data.

"The national average could eventually bottom out between $3.35 and $3.55 over the next few weeks," said Patrick De Haan, head of petroleum analysis at GasBuddy.

Some individual states have also moved to suspend gas taxes, providing another tailwind for consumers. North Carolina, Indiana, and Ohio are among the states that have temporarily suspended their gas taxes, helping push prices below the $3 threshold.

"GasBuddy now tracking 4 states with average gas prices at $2.999/gal or lower — all but one of which have gas tax suspensions. NC, IN, OK, and OH are all below $3 this morning," De Haan wrote in a social media post on Tuesday.

Brent crude (BZ=F) futures rose modestly to hover near $87 a barrel on Tuesday, while US West Texas Intermediate crude (CL=F) futures traded above $80. Oil prices have tumbled more than 22% over the past month as signs of diplomacy and easing political tensions have outweighed ongoing shipping risks through the Strait of Hormuz.
