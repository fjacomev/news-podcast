Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom lead chip stocks lower as AI trade comes under pressure

2026-11-25

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/nvidia-amd-broadcom-lead-chip-stocks-lower-as-ai-trade-comes-under-pressure-141635463.html

Nvidia (NVDA) stock retreated 6% on Tuesday, giving back some of its 31% surge from the prior session after the board of the AI chip giant reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. Peers AMD (AMD) and Broadcom (AVGO) also declined from Monday's rebound as the overall AI trade came under renewed pressure.

Shares of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSM), which manufactures Nvidia chips, and equipment maker ASML (ASML) fell more than 3% each, while chip designer Arm Holdings (ARM) was down 8%.

Shares of memory chipmaker Micron Technology (MU) and peer SK Hynix (SKHY) also dropped roughly 10%, giving back some of Monday's double-digit percentage gains. Storage and memory players Sandisk (SNDK), Seagate (STX), and Western Digital (WDC) all fell.

Other areas of the AI trade retreated, including the stocks of neocloud providers CoreWeave (CRWV) and Nebius.
