Apple gains smartphone share as memory crunch squeezes budget Android phones

2026-11-27

Por Rani Molla

https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/apple-gains-smartphone-share-as-memory-crunch-squeezes-budget-android-phones-134124966.html

Apple's share of US smartphone shipments jumped to 57% in the third quarter from 50% a year earlier as rising memory costs squeezed makers of budget Android phones, according to Counterpoint Research. Overall US smartphone shipments fell 9% year over year, and Apple was the only major phone maker whose shipments didn't decline.

Apple (AAPL) raised US prices by $100 on its iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max and on older models it still sells. The increase on the new models was smaller than some analysts had expected. Apple's scale gives it leverage with suppliers, while budget Android makers have thinner margins and less room to absorb rising costs.

Demand from AI data centers is helping to drive up memory costs and, by extension, phone prices — and the situation isn't expected to ease anytime soon.

Motorola took the biggest hit, with its share of US shipments falling to 8% from 12% a year earlier as higher memory costs squeezed its budget-heavy lineup. Google (GOOG) share slipped to 6% from 7% despite the launch of the Pixel 11. The new phone starts at $899, $100 above the Pixel 10's starting price, but came with double the storage.

Smartphone sales at the three largest US carriers fell 4% year over year in the third quarter. Counterpoint expects sales to remain weak in the holiday quarter, typically the strongest of the year, and expects carriers to be more selective about promotions.

"Consumers are timing their purchases around the best deals more than ever, if they buy at all," Counterpoint senior analyst Maurice Klaehne said. "This year's holiday sales cycle will look quite different from usual."
