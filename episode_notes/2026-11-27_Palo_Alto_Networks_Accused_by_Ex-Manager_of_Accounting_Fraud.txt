Palo Alto Networks Accused by Ex-Manager of Accounting Fraud

2026-11-27

Por Chris Dolmetsch; Peter Blumberg

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/palo-alto-networks-accused-ex-211701467.html

(Bloomberg) -- Palo Alto Networks Inc., which last week announced a $4.5 billion AI acquisition, was sued by a former executive over claims he was fired for fighting back against accounting fraud at the cybersecurity company.

Dan McGill, a former regional vice president, alleged in a complaint filed Friday in New York that he called attention to efforts to misrepresent transactions as booked sales in order to boost earnings.

The suit claims senior leadership directly endorsed practices meant to artificially inflate revenue by inducing resellers to prematurely issue purchase orders that hadn't been formally approved by customers. McGill says his bosses ginned up a story about him getting into an altercation with an employee of a competitor as a reason to terminate him.

"These allegations from a disgruntled former employee are completely meritless," company spokesperson Allie Cefalo said in a statement. "Palo Alto Networks operates with absolute integrity and holds itself and its employees to the highest ethical standards."

The Santa Clara, California-based company said last week that it's buying Applied Compute, which runs a platform for training and improving customized, open-weight artificial intelligence models and agents. The deal is expected to close in the fiscal third quarter of 2027.

Last quarter, Palo Alto warned that its shift toward more software subscription products was pushing cloud computing costs higher and weighing on gross margins. Chief Financial Officer Dipak Golechha said those costs were expected to grow faster than revenue in the 2027 fiscal year.

The case is McGill v. Palo Alto Networks Inc., 26-cv-10391, US District Court, Southern District of New York (Manhattan).
