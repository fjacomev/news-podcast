SpaceX, Elon Musk to give one share each to active Trump Accounts

2026-11-27

Por Ines Ferré

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/spacex-elon-musk-to-give-one-share-each-to-active-trump-accounts-205719872.html

SpaceX (SPCX) said Wednesday it will give one share of its Class A stock to every active Trump Account, with CEO Elon Musk personally matching the company's contribution.

"SpaceX is building the infrastructure of the future and working to make life multiplanetary," the company said in a statement. "We believe ownership of the company creating these systems should extend to the next generation of Americans for their long-term benefit."

SpaceX shares rose 1% in after-hours trading on Wednesday.

SpaceX also said president and COO Gwynne Shotwell had separately announced a personal gift of more than 2 million shares.

Trump Accounts are tax-advantaged investment accounts for children, created under the president's 2025 tax law. The program officially launched in July, with the government providing a one-time $1,000 contribution for eligible children born from 2025 through 2028.

Last month, the White House said nearly 70 million Trump Accounts had been created, including more than 60 million through automatic enrollment.

Since the July 4 launch, more than $4.5 billion has been deposited, including $1.3 billion in $1,000 government seed contributions, according to the White House.

Other public companies have also pledged funds to the accounts. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said in a CNBC interview last week that an Anthropic (ANT) co-founder will make a "large gift" to Trump Accounts in the coming weeks.

"We believe that it's important for these great entrepreneurs to share their bounty," he added.
