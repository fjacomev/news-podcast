SpaceX stock rises after Musk pledges annual share giveaway for kids

2026-11-27

Por Rani Molla

https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/article/spacex-stock-rises-after-musk-pledges-annual-share-giveaway-for-kids-140708762.html

SpaceX (SPCX) stock rose about 3% Friday after CEO Elon Musk said on X Thursday that he would give "all kids in America" another share of the company each year.

On Wednesday, Musk, who also runs Tesla (TSLA), had previously promised that "every child in America" would receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from him. SpaceX said its gift would go to currently active Trump Accounts and that Musk would match it. Musk didn't say whether his annual giveaway would follow the same eligibility rules.

Trump Accounts are available to eligible minors with a Social Security number. The 2025–2028 birth window applies to the federal government's one-time $1,000 contribution.

SpaceX shares were trading about 12% below their $150 opening trade in June as of Friday morning. The stock has come under pressure amid a broader sell-off in AI stocks and concerns that a potential merger with Tesla (TSLA) could dilute SpaceX shareholders.

SpaceX's AI division, which it calls Super Intelligence, accounted for just over half of the company's revenue and more than 80% of its capital spending in the third quarter.
