Tesla and SpaceX stocks sink as merger concerns mount

2026-11-27

Por Rani Molla

https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/tesla-and-spacex-stocks-sink-as-merger-concerns-mount-154159372.html

Tesla (TSLA) and SpaceX (SPCX) shares were particularly hard-hit in Wednesday's broader market sell-off amid growing investor pushback over a potential merger and another delay in European regulatory approval for Tesla's Full Self-Driving software. SpaceX stock fell 8% while Tesla shares were down nearly 6% in early trading as the rout in AI-related stocks continued. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC) was down 4%.

The two companies have enlisted five directors and three investment banks to help move forward what would be the biggest merger in history. But some Tesla investors are questioning how much shareholders stand to benefit from combining the two Elon Musk-led companies.

Gary Black, managing partner of The Future Fund, wrote on X Wednesday that even if SpaceX were to acquire Tesla at a 25% premium in an all-stock deal, Tesla shareholders could see those gains evaporate as share dilution puts pressure on SpaceX's stock.

Under that scenario, Black estimates SpaceX would have to issue roughly 52% more shares to acquire Tesla. Any resulting decline in SpaceX's stock price would reduce the value of the deal for Tesla shareholders.

The companies have yet to announce a deal or disclose its potential terms.

There could be an upside for SpaceX investors, however. JPMorgan analysts said Tuesday that a merger with Tesla could accelerate SpaceX's entry into the S&P 500 (^GSPC), prompting index funds tracking the benchmark to buy shares in the company.

Meanwhile, Tesla's efforts to expand its driver-assistance abilities in Europe hit another snag Wednesday.

A European Union meeting expected to consider approval of Tesla's assisted-driving technology across the bloc has been postponed until Dec. 15, Reuters reported, citing a European Commission spokesperson. Musk has previously said that approval of the software was necessary to turn around Tesla's declining car sales in Europe.
